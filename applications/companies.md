# Companies to Apply To — Summer 2027 Internships

## How to Use This List

1. Research each company BEFORE applying
2. Find 1 employee on LinkedIn who can refer you
3. Check if they have DevOps/SRE-specific internships
4. Apply within 24h of finding the listing

---

## 🔴 Tier 1: Big Tech (Apply Day 1 — August 2026)

### Google
- **Site:** careers.google.com | levels.fyi
- **Roles:** Site Reliability Engineer Intern, Cloud Intern, DevOps Intern
- **Locations:** NYC, Mountain View, Seattle
- **Pipeline:** googool.com/join → Technical → Intern
- **Salary:** ~$7,500-9,500/month (NYC)
- **Pro tip:** Google's interview process is 3 rounds. Practice DP and graphs on LeetCode.
- **Referral:** Find SREs on LinkedIn, ask for referral directly

### Meta
- **Site:** careers.metameats.com | levels.fyi
- **Roles:** Production Engineer Intern, Software Engineer Intern (DevOps)
- **Locations:** NYC, Menlo Park, Seattle
- **Pipeline:** 1 OA + 2 technical rounds
- **Salary:** ~$8,000-10,000/month
- **Pro tip:** Meta's PE role is DevOps-heavy. Focus on systems design.

### Amazon
- **Site:** amazon.jobs | levels.fyi
- **Roles:** SDE Intern, DevOps Engineer Intern, SRE Intern
- **Locations:** NYC (Hudson Yards), Arlington, Seattle
- **Pipeline:** 2 OAs + 2 rounds
- **Salary:** ~$6,500-8,500/month + housing
- **Pro tip:** Amazon is the LARGEST hirer of DevOps interns. Apply early. Bar is lower than Google/Meta.
- **Known for:** 14 leadership principles. Know them.

### Microsoft
- **Site:** careers.microsoft.com | levels.fyi
- **Roles:** Software Engineer Intern (Azure), DevOps/SRE Intern
- **Locations:** Redmond, NYC, Remote
- **Pipeline:** 1 OA + 2-3 rounds
- **Salary:** ~$6,000-8,000/month + housing
- **Pro tip:** Azure DevOps is a huge product. Mention Azure experience if you have any.

### Apple
- **Site:** apple.com/jobs | levels.fyi
- **Roles:** Systems Engineer Intern, macOS Intern
- **Locations:** Cupertino, NYC
- **Pipeline:** 2-3 rounds (systems design heavy)
- **Salary:** ~$6,500-9,000/month
- **Pro tip:** Apple cares about hardware knowledge. Mention your homelab.
